## Attendance at the point of work

DRP Smart Attendance is a mobile-first attendance tool for people who are not sitting at a desk. Biometric authentication establishes identity; location provides context; and offline logging keeps the app usable when the network is not available.

The reporting layer turns individual check-ins into something a team can review. Exports support the formats that businesses already use instead of forcing everyone into a new dashboard.

## Designing for unreliable conditions

The app has to handle failed authentication, missing location permissions, and sessions that begin offline and sync later. Those states are treated as normal parts of the workflow rather than exceptions hidden behind a generic error.

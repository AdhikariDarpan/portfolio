## A newsroom, not a demo

Bishwo Darpan is a live bilingual news and travel platform. The work went beyond building a public article page: the platform needed a usable editorial workflow, controlled access for different staff roles, a category system that could grow, and enough structure for search engines to understand the publication.

I owned the project from the first schema decision through deployment. The application is built with Laravel and Livewire, with Vue where a richer interaction made sense, and MySQL as the source of truth.

## Decisions that mattered

The content model separates articles, categories, navigation, and users while keeping publishing operations simple for a small editorial team. Scheduled publishing, drafts, and role-aware navigation make the admin experience predictable without turning it into a separate product.

For readers, the public side focuses on fast article delivery, clean URLs, localized labels, and strong metadata. Analytics are deliberately practical: what was published, what was read, which categories are working, and who is engaging with the content.

## Running in production

The platform runs on constrained shared hosting, so deployment, file handling, caching, and backups were designed around those limits rather than assuming a managed cloud environment. That constraint shaped the architecture as much as the feature list.

## Where OCR alone breaks down

Billing documents are not clean inputs. They are photographed at an angle, printed on faded paper, and filled with numbers that look similar. Running OCR across the whole image produces a crowd of plausible but unreliable values.

This project narrows the problem with detection. A YOLO-based model locates the relevant regions, and OCR is applied only where it matters. That makes the output easier to validate and gives the pipeline a way to reject a document when the region itself cannot be found.

## The reconciliation layer is the product

The extractor does not exist in isolation. It is one stage in a larger billing workflow: extracted values are normalized, checked against expected relationships, and passed to reconciliation. The most valuable engineering work was in the boundaries between those stages, including confidence handling and making uncertain results visible to an operator.

The implementation is private because it is part of a production client system. The case study focuses on the computer-vision and integration approach without exposing client details.

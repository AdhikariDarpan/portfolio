## A clinic day, modeled honestly

The platform was designed around the actual sequence of a clinic visit: book an appointment, assign an available doctor, record the consultation, issue a prescription, and make any paid reports accessible to the patient.

Django provided the structure for those related records and permissions. The interface separates the concerns by role while keeping the underlying patient history connected.

## Payments with a purpose

Payment integration was not added as an isolated checkout. It controls access to specific reports, which meant the transaction state had to be trustworthy and visible in both the patient and administrative views. Email notifications close the loop by giving patients a clear next step after booking or payment.

The public repository contains the reference implementation of the platform.

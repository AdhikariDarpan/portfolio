## A small record that stays useful

Most attendance tools are built for HR departments. This one is built for an individual who wants a reliable record of working hours without a spreadsheet that gets forgotten.

The app deliberately keeps the interaction small: authenticate, check in, check out, and review the history. Firebase handles persistence so the record is not tied to a single device.

## Trust the timeline

The data model preserves individual sessions rather than only a daily total, which makes it possible to detect missing check-outs, compare work patterns, and export the raw record. The UI keeps the important state visible: whether a session is open and what was recorded today.

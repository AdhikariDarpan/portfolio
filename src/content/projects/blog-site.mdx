## A small publishing product

This blog platform was built as a complete publishing loop: write an article, organize it, publish it, and let readers interact without a heavy frontend framework. PHP and MySQL keep the stack approachable, while AJAX handles the parts that should not require a page reload.

SEO was treated as part of the content model rather than an afterthought. Articles have stable slugs, meaningful metadata, and a structure that search engines can parse.

## Progressive enhancement

The PWA layer improves repeat visits without making the site unusable when JavaScript or service-worker support is limited. Likes and comments enhance the page; the article itself remains readable without them.

The project is a useful snapshot of building a practical publishing experience with a deliberately small toolchain.

## A risk signal, not a legal verdict

Image copyright detection is not something a small pipeline can answer definitively. This project takes a more honest approach: collect several weak signals, combine them into a risk score, and show the reasoning.

The pipeline inspects EXIF metadata, generates perceptual hashes, and compares visual features to detect duplicates or edited variants. Missing metadata, unusual camera information, and strong similarity to known images all contribute to the score.

## Keep the uncertainty visible

The most useful part of the interface is the signal breakdown. A user can see why an image was flagged and decide what to do next, rather than trusting a black-box percentage.

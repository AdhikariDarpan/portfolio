## A gallery that stays out of the way

DRP Gallery is a personal media browser built around speed and privacy. Images, video, and audio share one interface, with folder organization and fullscreen playback that feel native.

The Flutter implementation uses `video_player`, `just_audio`, and cached image loading to keep transitions fast. The goal was not to add every possible media feature, but to make the common path of opening, browsing, playing, and moving on feel immediate.

## Local by default

The app is designed for local media and does not require an account or cloud service. That makes storage, indexing, and memory management the important engineering concerns, especially on devices with large media libraries.

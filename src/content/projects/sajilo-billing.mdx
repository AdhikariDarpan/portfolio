## Billing software for a small shop

Sajilo Billing is designed around the rhythm of a small business: create an invoice, keep a customer record, review the day, and understand what is selling. The mobile app keeps those tasks close at hand, while a Go backend handles the parts that need a durable service boundary.

The product avoids enterprise complexity. Reports are practical, customer management is lightweight, and the primary flows are reachable without navigating a deep settings tree.

## Connected, but not fragile

An offline-first approach keeps billing usable when the network is unreliable. The data model is designed to reconcile local work with the backend rather than treating connectivity as an all-or-nothing condition.
